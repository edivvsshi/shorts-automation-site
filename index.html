Shorts Automation

Shorts Automation is a private tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel ed1vvs to produce and publish one vertical Short per day, mainly the animated interactive series „TĂCEREA”.

What it does

  • Renders original 2D animated episodes (characters, scenes, music and voice-over are generated by the tool).
  • Uploads each episode to the owner's own YouTube channel and schedules its publication time.
  • Reads the comments of the previous episode to count viewer votes (A or B); the winning option decides what happens in the next episode.
  • Reads view counts of the channel's own videos to choose the best daily publishing time.

Who can use it

Only the channel owner. The tool is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's account. Viewers only interact with the channel through normal YouTube comments.
